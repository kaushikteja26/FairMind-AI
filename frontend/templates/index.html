⏱️ Step 6: Temporal Bias Drift Detection
★ PAPER-LEVEL FEATURE
🔬 What This Does

Standard bias audits are one-shot snapshots. But AI bias is not static — models become more discriminatory over time as real-world data drifts. This feature detects whether your model's bias is getting worse, improving, or stable across time periods. Directly addresses EU AI Act Article 9 (continuous monitoring requirement). No other free, no-code tool does this.
